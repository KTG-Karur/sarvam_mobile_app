Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Sarvam ("Sarvam", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information when you use the Sarvam mobile application (package com.ktg.sarvam) and the Sarvam Microfinance ERP web application at sarvam.nidhimfi.com (together, the "App").

The App is a business tool used by Sarvam's employees (field officers, branch managers, area managers and administrators) to manage microfinance operations, including member enrolment, KYC, loan processing, disbursement, collections, attendance and field-staff management. Members and loan applicants do not use the App directly. Their information is entered by Sarvam staff during loan processing.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2

Information We Collect

2.1 Employee account information

  • Name, employee ID, designation, branch, mobile number and email address.
  • Login credentials, MPIN and one-time passwords (OTP) used for sign-in.
  • Attendance records (punch-in / punch-out time), leave applications and supporting documents.

2.2 Member and loan applicant information

To process microfinance loans, staff enter the following information about members, co-applicants, spouses and nominees:

  • Name, date of birth, gender, marital status, religion, caste category and relationship details.
  • Mobile number and residential address.
  • Identity and address proof details such as Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID and ration card numbers.
  • Occupation, income, economic activity and household details.
  • Bank account number, account type and IFSC code for loan disbursement.
  • Nominee details, including name, age, gender, date of birth and mobile number.
  • Loan, repayment and collection records.

2.3 Location information

The App collects precise (GPS) and approximate location data, including in the background, even when the App is closed or not in use, while an employee is punched in for duty.

We collect location data for the following purposes:

  • Attendance: to record the employee's location at punch-in and punch-out.
  • Live tracking of field staff (background location): after an employee punches in, the App records their location periodically, even when the App is minimised, closed or not in use. A persistent notification is shown while this is active. We use it to verify field visits, calculate distance travelled and show the employee's route to authorised managers. A new location point is recorded only after the device has moved a minimum distance. The device's battery level is sent along with each location update. Tracking stops automatically when the employee punches out, and no location is collected in the background while the employee is punched out.
  • Geotagging of field activities: to record where member enrolment, KYC, house verification, centre creation, loan disbursement and collection entries took place. This confirms that the visit happened at the stated address.
  • Distance calculation: to calculate the distance between a branch and a centre.

Location data is sent securely to Sarvam's servers. It is used only for the purposes above and is never sold or used for advertising.

2.4 Camera, photos and documents

  • Camera: used to photograph KYC documents, member and applicant photos, house verification photos, collection receipts and disbursement records, and for face attendance.
  • Photos and files: when you choose to upload an existing photo or document, the App uses the Android system photo picker or file picker. The App can only access the specific items you select. It does not read your photo gallery or files in general.
  • Document text recognition: text on KYC documents may be read on the device (using Google ML Kit) to fill in form fields automatically. This processing happens on the device.

2.5 Face data (face attendance)

With the employee's consent, the App uses the front camera for face attendance. During face enrolment, the App creates a biometric face template (a set of numbers that represents facial features). This template and an enrolment photo are sent to Sarvam's servers. When an employee punches in or out, a live face check (including a liveness check) is compared against the enrolled template to confirm identity. Face detection runs on the device. Face data is used only to verify employee attendance. It is never used for any other purpose and is never shared with third parties. Employees may ask for their face data to be deleted at any time (see Section 8).

2.6 Device and technical information

  • Device model, operating system version and an app-generated device identifier. We use these to secure the employee's account on a trusted device and to prevent attendance fraud.
  • Network connection status and battery level. We use these to keep live tracking and uploads reliable.
  • OTP auto-fill: the App uses Google's SMS Retriever API to read only the OTP message sent to it. The App does not request permission to read your SMS messages.
  • Fingerprint or device biometric unlock is handled by your device. The App never receives your fingerprint data.
3

How We Use Information

  • To process, approve, disburse and service microfinance loans.
  • To verify the identity and address of members and applicants (KYC).
  • To record employee attendance and verify field visits.
  • To prevent fraud, secure accounts and keep the App reliable.
  • To generate operational, accounting and regulatory reports.
  • To comply with applicable laws, regulations and requests from authorities.
4

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only in the following cases:

  • Within Sarvam: with authorised staff who need it for their role. For example, managers can view the attendance and routes of the field staff they supervise.
  • Service providers: with hosting, mapping (Google Maps) and infrastructure providers that run the App on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal and regulatory: with regulators, credit information companies, law enforcement or other authorities where required by law or for loan processing as permitted by law.
5

Data Storage and Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on secured servers. Sensitive information on the device, such as session tokens and face data, is kept in encrypted secure storage. Access is limited according to each user's role. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information.

6

Data Retention

Personal and loan information is kept for as long as it is needed for the purposes above and for the periods required by applicable financial, tax and regulatory laws. Location and attendance records are kept for as long as they are needed for HR, payroll and audit purposes. Data that is no longer required is deleted or anonymised.

7

Permissions Used by the App

Location

Precise, approximate and background. Used for attendance, live tracking while punched in, and geotagging (see Section 2.3).

Camera

Documents, member photos and face attendance.

Notifications

The live-tracking notification and app alerts.

Biometric

Optional fingerprint or face unlock using your device's biometrics.

Foreground service & battery

Foreground service and battery optimisation exemption keep live tracking running while punched in.

You can turn off any permission in your device's Settings, under Apps › Sarvam › Permissions. Turning off location or camera will stop the features that depend on them, such as attendance, live tracking and document capture.

8

Your Rights and Data Deletion

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent, including consent for face data and location tracking. To do so, email us at the address below. We will respond within a reasonable time. Some information may need to be kept where the law requires it, for example records of active or closed loans.

9

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use only by Sarvam employees aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children through the App. Where a minor's details are recorded as a nominee, this is done only as part of a loan application submitted by an adult member.
