DATA QUALITY CONSIDERATIONS
================================================================================

VALIDATION RULES THAT MUST BE APPLIED:
  • Phone: 10 digits (with optional +91, 91, or 0 prefix)
  • Aadhaar: Exactly 12 digits
  • PAN: Format ABCDE1234F (5 letters, 4 digits, 1 letter)
  • IFSC: Format SBIN0001234 (4 letters, 0, 6 alphanumeric)
  • Bank Account: 9-18 digits
  • Pincode: Exactly 6 digits
  • Age: 18-100 for clients
  • Loan Amount: Minimum ₹1,000
  • Interest Rate: 0-100%
  • Tenure: 1-120 months

FIELDS WITH DEPENDENCIES:
  • Loan details depend on LoanProduct + LoanPurpose lookups
  • Client depends on Center, Branch existence
  • Loan depends on Client, Center, Branch, Product, Purpose
  • Transactions depend on Loan existence
  • Outstanding balances depend on LoanSchedule + Transactions
